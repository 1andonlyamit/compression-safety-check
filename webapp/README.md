# Compression Safety Check · Tiny Aya

An interactive tool from my Lexsi AI Safety and Alignment Hackathon project (Track 2b).

- **Check a version:** pick a compressed version of Tiny Aya Fire and a test question. You see its real recorded answer, a safety grade from A to F, the reasons behind the grade, and what to do next.
- **Repair:** flip a switch to apply a 28 MB weight-space patch to a badly compressed model. The broken answer becomes a proper one, and you can see where in the model the patch edits.
- **Insights, Method, Experiments:** every version's grade, patch-size results, why the model broke, and a toy that rounds real Tiny Aya weights so you can see the damage.

Every answer shown was generated by the real models during the study: 61 configurations, 700 questions each, judged by two AI judges. Replies to harmful requests are published only when the model cleanly refused.

Built with Lexsi Labs' **SafeTune**, **CircuitKIT** and **AuditKIT**. The model is Cohere Labs' **Tiny Aya Fire** (CC-BY-NC-4.0). Non-commercial use only, with credit to Cohere Labs.
